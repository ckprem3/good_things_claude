# Good Things: 2026-07-24

1. Amazon Deforestation Hits 10-Year Low: Brazil's Amazon deforestation fell to its lowest level in a decade in the first half of 2026, with clearing down 38% compared to the same period in 2025 — potentially the lowest rate since record-keeping began in 1988 [https://news.mongabay.com/short-article/2026/07/amazon-deforestation-falls-to-10-year-low-in-first-half-of-2026/]

2. Global Hunger Declines for Third Straight Year: A new UN report found that 7.8% of the global population faced hunger in 2025, down from 8.1% in 2024 and 8.6% in 2022, with significant improvements across Asia, Latin America, and the Caribbean [https://www.who.int/news/item/21-07-2026-un-report--global-hunger-levels-ease-for-third-consecutive-year-as-regional-disparities-persist]

3. 4. Africa's Solar Energy Boom Breaks Records: On-grid solar installations across Africa in 2026 have already surpassed the total for all of 2025, marking a historic clean energy milestone driven by falling costs and new large-scale projects [https://www.africa-energy.com/live-data/article/african-2026-grid-solar-additions-surpass-those-2025]

5. Gentler Prostate Cancer Treatment Speeds Recovery: A major clinical trial (CAPTAIN) found that MRI-guided ultrasound ablation for prostate cancer lets patients go home the same day and return to normal activities far faster than traditional surgery, with no compromise on effectiveness [https://www.sirweb.org/for-press/new-study-shows-faster-recovery-with-minimally-invasive-prostate-cancer-treatment/]
