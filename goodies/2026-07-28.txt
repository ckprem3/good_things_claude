# Good Things: 2026-07-28

1. ISS Crew Returns Safely After 241 Days: NASA astronaut Chris Williams and Russian cosmonauts Sergei Kud-Sverchkov and Sergei Mikaev landed safely in Kazakhstan after completing a 241-day mission aboard the International Space Station, traveling over 102 million miles and orbiting Earth 3,856 times [https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/spacestation/2026/07/26/expedition-74-trio-returns-to-earth-inside-soyuz/]

2. World Nature Conservation Day: July 28 marks World Nature Conservation Day, a global occasion celebrating humanity's collective commitment to protecting biodiversity and sustainable stewardship of our planet's natural resources [https://www.awarenessdays.com/awareness-days-calendar/world-nature-conservation-day/]

3. European Bison Thriving Again: Europe's largest land mammal has made a remarkable comeback — wild European bison numbers have grown from just 2,500 to around 9,000 individuals over the past decade, spread across more than 40 herds, a century after the species vanished from nature [https://nationaltoday.com/us/ca/san-francisco/news/2026/04/10/european-bison-comeback-boosts-biodiversity-and-beyond/]

4. Ancient Cave Art Finally Dated: Scientists precisely dated the prehistoric paintings inside France's Font-de-Gaume cave for the first time, discovering charcoal in the black pigments that allowed radiocarbon dating — a breakthrough connecting us more clearly to our ancient ancestors [https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/top/science/]

5. Boy Reaches 100 Hours Reading to Shelter Animals: Nehemiah Turner became the first child in the history of the Jacksonville Humane Society's Pawsitive Reading program to reach 100 volunteer hours, spending over two years reading books to homeless shelter animals to help them feel loved and calm [https://www.sunnyskyz.com/good-news]
