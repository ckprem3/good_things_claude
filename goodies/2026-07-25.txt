# Good Things: 2026-07-25

1. Solar Becomes EU's Largest Power Source: For the first time ever, solar energy supplied 25% of all EU electricity in June 2026 — more than nuclear, gas, wind, or hydro — a record 52 TWh milestone marking a historic clean energy turning point. [https://electrek.co/2026/07/15/solar-just-became-europes-biggest-source-of-electricity-heres-the-milestone-it-hit/]

2. Teen Cowboy Lassoes Drowning Man to Safety: 14-year-old Jory Thomas of Montana sprinted along the Yellowstone River and roped a drowning man on his very first throw, using rodeo skills he'd been honing on his family's ranch — the county sheriff said Jory saved the man's life 100%. [https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/07/21/14-year-old-ranch-kid-ropes-drowning-man-on-yellowstone-river-saves-life/]

3. Malaria Vaccine Creator Wins Top European Honour: Irish scientist Sir Adrian Hill was crowned winner of the European Inventor Award 2026 for developing the R21/Matrix-M malaria vaccine, which offers 75–80% protection, costs just £2.50 a dose, and has already been rolled out across ~20 countries. [https://www.siliconrepublic.com/innovation/irish-developer-malaria-vaccine-wins-european-inventor-award-2026-healthcare]

4. Limiting Social Media Helps Teens Sleep and Thrive: A UK government-backed trial found that teenagers who restricted social media use reported sleeping better, concentrating more in class, feeling calmer, and spending more quality time with family — with overnight curfews proving especially effective. [https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/social-media-restriction-pilots-qualitative-research-with-13-to-17-year-olds-in-the-uk/social-media-intervention-research-2026-social-media-restriction-pilots-qualitative-research-with-13-to-17-year-olds-in-the-uk]

5. Mom and Son Graduate Together: Lissette Garcia and her son Jason both walked the stage at the University of Maryland Global Campus on the same day — Lissette having started her degree journey in 2018 — crossing the graduation stage side by side in a moment the family called "a massive accomplishment." [https://www.cbsnews.com/baltimore/news/maryland-mother-son-graduate-together-umgc/]
